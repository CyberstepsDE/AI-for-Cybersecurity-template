# Less is more

> **Load this when:** you are about to add a tool, a step, a finding or a paragraph.

## The rule

**Use the smallest set of tools, steps and words that answers the question.** Before
adding anything, answer: what question does this answer, and who acts on it?

## In practice

- **Tools.** Every tool you give the agent is something it can misuse or be tricked
  into using. Connect the tools the task needs and no more.
- **Steps.** Do not run a broad scan when one targeted read answers the question. More
  output is more to verify, not more certainty.
- **Findings.** A finding without an action for the reader is noise. Ten weak findings
  hide the one that matters.
- **Words.** A report is read by someone with little time. Lead with the verdict and its
  evidence; put the detail after it.

## Less is not thin

This is not a licence to skip evidence or to leave out what you did not check. Cut the
filler, keep the reasoning.
